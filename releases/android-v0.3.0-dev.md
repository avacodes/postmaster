Postmaster Android companion — preview release.

- Sign in to your workspace to manage contacts, conversations and senders.
- Connect this phone as an SMS sender and choose its SIM.
- Add an authorized Telegram account on this phone.
- Check permission and Express readiness settings in one place.
- Use the app in Arabic or English.

Download **postmaster.apk**. Install it over your existing compatible preview installation to retain your connections. Android 8.0 or later is required.

The phone, its permissions, internet connection and provider session must remain available. Express readiness is a current device check, not a delivery guarantee. WhatsApp sending from Android is not included.

Website and help: https://postmaster.avacodes.org
