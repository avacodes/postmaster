Postmaster PC sender — first preview for Windows x64 and Linux x64.

- Connect your computer to a Postmaster workspace using a one-use pairing token.
- Send through customer-owned Telegram and WhatsApp accounts, with provider sessions stored on your computer.
- Complete account setup in the local Arabic or English setup page.
- Review connection status and revoke the computer from your workspace when needed.

Download the ZIP for your operating system, verify its SHA-256 checksum and extract the entire archive. Follow the included README. Windows: run **postmaster-sender.exe**. Linux: make **postmaster-sender** executable and run it. Open **http://127.0.0.1:23527** on that same computer.

WhatsApp setup requires Docker and downloads a separate sending service. Keep the sender process, computer, internet connection and provider session available. This portable preview has no automatic updater or system-service installation. Connected status does not guarantee recipient delivery.

Setup guide: https://postmaster.avacodes.org/docs/senders
